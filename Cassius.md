## Class Moves
### Lay on Hands (CHA)
When you **touch someone, skin to skin, and pray for their well-being **, roll+CHA
* On a 10+ you heal 1d8 damage or remove one disease.
* On a 7–9, they are healed, but the damage or disease is transferred to you.
  * if healing yourself, you are healed but take -1 ongoing to lay on hands until you rest.

### Armored
Ignore the *clumsy* tag on armor 

### I Am the Law (CHA)
When you command with divine authority roll +CHA:
* 7+: They obey, flee, or attack
* 10+: +1 forward vs them
* 6-: they do as they please and you take -1 forward against them

### Quest
You swear a sacred mission and gain divine boons and burdens 

**Quest:**
> Purge the temple of chaos from the borderlands

**Boons:**
* Eyes that see in darkness
* A freedom from hunger, thirst, and sleep

**Vows:**
* Valor (forbidden: suffering an evil creature to live)
* Honor (forbidden: cowardly tactics and tricks)
